How tracking works

Request a pickup

Book a future collection date and you will receive a Pickup Request ID such as WL20491.

Collector is allotted

The portal allots a municipal vehicle and the status becomes "Collector Assigned".

Watch the vehicle

The vehicle moves along the sector route while distance and ETA count down to arrival.
