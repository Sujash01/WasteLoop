Our Purpose

About WasteLoop

WasteLoop supports cleaner cities through digital pickup requests, recycling guidance, and citizen participation.

Mission

To make waste collection, segregation, and recycling services accessible to every citizen through a transparent public portal.

Vision

To build cleaner urban and rural communities where recoverable waste returns to productive use.

Objectives

Enable scheduled pickup, improve segregation, reward verified recycling, and support local authorities with citizen-ready data.

Importance of Recycling

Recycling reduces landfill pressure, conserves raw materials, improves public hygiene, and creates a circular economy.

Government Initiative

The portal is designed in the spirit of national cleanliness and digital public service missions, ready for future backend integration.

Citizen Role

Citizens can segregate waste at source, request pickups, track status, and participate in local green campaigns.

WasteLoop

Academic prototype of a citizen waste management portal. Not an official Government of India service.
