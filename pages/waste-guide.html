Waste Categories

    Why segregate at source?

    Wet, dry, hazardous and e-waste streams that are mixed together cannot be recycled — they end up in landfill. Segregating at home keeps recyclables clean and protects waste workers.

    Green Points

    Every completed pickup credits Green Points based on the waste category. Points are awarded automatically by the portal when a collector marks your request Completed.
